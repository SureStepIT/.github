## Navigator

**Regulatory change management for compliance teams. A full compliance bench for a team of any size.**

Navigator watches the rules, tells you which changes matter to your business, and drafts the updates to your controls, policies and procedures. AI does the reading and the first draft. Your people make the decisions, and every one is on the record.

### What it does

- **Drafts the response.** Controls, policies and procedures, each tied to the rule text it answers and approved by a person.
- **Assesses regulatory change.** Reads each alert, judges whether it applies to you, and gives the reason in plain language.
- **Maps the impact** across business areas, processes, controls and policies.
- **Builds the action plan.** Changes, work items and one inbox.
- **Keeps the proof.** Citations, linked evidence, signed decision records, and audit-ready reports and packages.

### How it works

| Step | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Map it** | Your obligations, policies and controls, linked and versioned in one place. |
| **Watch it** | A live feed from 150+ regulators, every day, with alerts scored for how much they matter to your business. |
| **Prove it** | Every draft cites the rule text. Evidence is linked to the controls it supports. Decisions are signed records. |

### Good to know

- **You stay in control.** You choose what runs on its own. It always stops where you want a person to decide, and anything going into force needs a person's approval.
- **Answers come with a citation.** Every answer is read from, and cited to, the actual rule text, including the most common frameworks: NIST CSF, SOC 2, ISO 27001, SOX, OSFI, DORA and PCI DSS.
- **Start anywhere, keep what you have.** Use Navigator on its own, or connect it to the GRC system you already have and keep both in step.
- **Your choice of AI.** The standard engine, your own AI provider key, or a private deployment.
- **Setup.** A standard implementation takes about two weeks. More or custom datasets extend that.
